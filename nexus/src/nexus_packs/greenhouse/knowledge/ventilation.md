# Ventilation

The greenhouse has a single roof vent. It opens from 0 percent (closed) to 100 percent.

## Opening the vent

Open the vent to 30 percent when the inside temperature passes 28 degrees Celsius, and fully
when it passes 32 degrees Celsius. Close the vent when the wind speed is above 50 km/h, because
the hinge is not rated for stronger gusts.

## Humidity

Keep the relative humidity between 60 and 80 percent. Above 85 percent, open the vent to at
least 20 percent for an hour to prevent mould.
