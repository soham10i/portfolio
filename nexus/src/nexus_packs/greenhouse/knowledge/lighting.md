# Lighting

Each zone has its own grow lights, switched on or off.

## Schedule

The grow lights run for 16 hours a day, from 06:00 to 22:00. Seedlings in zone 1 get 18 hours,
from 05:00 to 23:00.

## Night and maintenance

Switch off the grow lights of a zone before anyone works in it at night, so the lamps are cool
to the touch. The lamps are LED panels rated for 50000 hours.
