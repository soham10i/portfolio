# Irrigation

Each zone is watered through its own drip line. Watering cannot be undone, so every watering run
needs a human approval.

## Amounts

A single watering run delivers between 0.5 and 20 litres to one zone. Tomatoes need about 4
litres per zone each morning; herbs in zone 4 need 1.5 litres every second day. Seedlings in
zone 1 are misted by hand and are not watered through the drip line.

## Limits

No more than 20 watering runs are allowed per minute across the greenhouse. The water tank holds
500 litres and is refilled every Monday.
